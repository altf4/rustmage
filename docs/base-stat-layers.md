## Runtime and search

Characteristic definitions apply in layer7a, then exact-object base setters in
7b, then growth and counters in7c, followed by additive attachment/static bonuses.
Power and toughness choose their latest applicable setter independently. One-sided
setters preserve the other axis. Overwritten printed/CDAs do not produce a false
intermediate overflow; final additive arithmetic remains checked and transactional.
Noncreatures retain their existing zero-stat views.

Static self setters capture their battlefield-entry timestamp even while their
condition is false. Own-turn and graveyard conditions use the current controller
and current earlier-layer facts without acquiring a later timestamp. Floating
setters capture fixed recipients on resolution and keep their timestamp when the
source leaves. They cannot follow bounce/reentry or a stale source incarnation.
Cursed Roles use the same timestamp clock, so later attachments and later spell
setters interact in actual timestamp order. Source conditions that could depend
on same-layer power/toughness, type/name/color replacement, ability removal, copies
and switches remain gated until their full rules are implemented.

Sparse records live in the existing optional Role allocation: there is no added
object field or default-state pointer. State bytes count the initialized vector
and records once. Observations expose owned records in both views; independent
clones remain usable after their parent is dropped. Cleanup removes floating
setters before state actions. Central departure removes records only after LKI
capture; the simultaneous-movement path captures every pre-event view first.
