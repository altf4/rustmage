The engine checks immutable `CastFilter` metadata against the chosen spell definition. The shared battlefield-static index avoids scanning the whole catalogue or adding mutable permission graphs to each game. Source departure removes future permissions through the existing source-index lifecycle. A spell already paid and on the stack remains legal after the timing source departs. The metadata is copied to generated offspring definitions with their other immutable abilities.

Timing permission is independent of zone permission and funding. It does not allow casting a card from an otherwise unavailable zone, bypass mana or target requirements, alter the number/timing of land plays, enable Plot special actions at other times, or bypass the explicit sorcery timing restriction for casting a plotted card. A physical front face's Flash does not transfer to its chosen Sorcery inset. Sorcery Adventures can use the permission, while their normal non-Dragon creature face still needs its own legal casting timing even after Adventure grants exile play.
