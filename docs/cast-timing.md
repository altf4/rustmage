# Chosen-spell casting timing

> Historical component evidence: counts, schemas, hashes and pending gates below refer to that component branch. The authoritative combined delivery is [PR275](review-pr-275.md).

Whirlwing Stormbrood's complete normal body supplies a battlefield static permission for Sorcery spells and Dragon spells. Its printed Flash and Flying remain intrinsic keywords. Dynamic Soar is a separate chosen Sorcery Omen face, costs {2}{G}, targets a creature the caster controls, puts three +1/+1 counters on it, and shuffles the same physical card into its owner's library on successful resolution.

The engine checks immutable `CastFilter` metadata against the chosen spell definition. The shared battlefield-static index avoids scanning the whole catalogue or adding mutable permission graphs to each game. Source departure removes future permissions through the existing source-index lifecycle. A spell already paid and on the stack remains legal after the timing source departs. The metadata is copied to generated offspring definitions with their other immutable abilities.

Timing permission is independent of zone permission and funding. It does not allow casting a card from an otherwise unavailable zone, bypass mana or target requirements, alter the number/timing of land plays, enable Plot special actions at other times, or bypass the explicit sorcery timing restriction for casting a plotted card. A physical front face's Flash does not transfer to its chosen Sorcery inset. Sorcery Adventures can use the permission, while their normal non-Dragon creature face still needs its own legal casting timing even after Adventure grants exile play.

This uses the engine's current owner/controller model for admitted own-card plays; the full ownership/control foundation remains tracked in issue #265. The permission is an as-though effect, not an added Flash keyword. Rules basis: Comprehensive Rules 601.3b, 609.4 and 702.8; pinned Java `WhirlwingStormbrood` and `CastAsThoughItHadFlashAllEffect` inspect the spell ability's chosen characteristics and the source controller. Full-file hashes remain in `omen-admissions.json`.

The new compiler route requires an exact complete permission sentence on a permanent shape and an actual compiled permanent effect. Unknown residual parentheticals cannot enter inherited stripping templates; rejected timing instructions cannot be revived by legacy name selectors. Nonpermanent spell instructions need their own duration-bearing resolution program and remain gated by this route.
