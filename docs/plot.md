# Plot special actions and later free casting

Issue [240](https://github.com/altf4/rustmage/issues/240). The standalone branch implements 1,669 of the 5,164 pinned Standard identities, with 80 immutable token factories and observation schema70. Thirteen complete bodies are admitted: Beastbond Outcaster, Blacksnag Buzzard, Cunning Coyote, Djinn of Fool's Fall, Irascible Wolverine, Loan Shark, Slickshot Show-Off, Spinewoods Paladin, Stagecoach Security, Stingerback Terror, Demonic Ruckus, Rictus Robber and Rise of the Varmints. The original ten candidates are included; the three natural additional bodies have complete supported residuals rather than a name-specific admission exception.

Under [CR702.170 and116.2k/116.3](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.pdf), `AnnouncePlot {card}` starts a paid special action during the owner's main phase with priority and an empty stack. The exact card remains in Hand through the mana window. `PayPlot {payment}` spends actual floating mana and commits its next exact incarnation to face-up Exile. The actor retains priority. The special action consumes no stack identity, casts no spell, announces no ordinary activated ability, and does not increment cast history. Actual mana abilities still cause their genuine tap and sacrifice events.

`plot_payment: {object, source_card, controller, cost}` is visible only to its paying actor. The public `plotted_cards: [{object, owner, plotted_turn}]` registry records the committed permissions identically for both viewers. The registry is sparse and State-owned, with no new per-object flag or global mutable table. A plotted exact incarnation may be cast with `AnnouncePlotted {card}` on a strictly later turn, only during its owner's main phase with an empty stack. Intrinsic flash cannot override this sorcery restriction. Its ordinary owned spell packet has `cast.origin = Exile` and `cast.method = Plot`. It uses a zero base mana cost, retaining printed mana value/colors, targets and actual additional costs; printed casting X is zero. Effect quantities named X, such as Rise of the Varmints' live graveyard count, retain their own semantics.

A permission is consumed on every departure from Exile, whether cast, moved to Hand or otherwise moved. A returning same-ID card is a new incarnation and gains no old permission. The free alternative is separate from Flashback and creates no forced-exile stack-departure marker. Irascible Wolverine's ordinary paid, until-end-of-turn play permission remains independent: it can permit a same-turn paid cast, while a plotted permission still forbids the same-turn free cast. An actual departure consumes both exact rights.

Plotting does not require legal spell targets because it is not casting. Later free casting does. Demonic Ruckus retains enchant-creature legality, its full +1/+1/menace/trample grant and battlefield-to-graveyard draw. Coyote retains another-owned-creature targeting, Haste and temporary cleanup. Stagecoach buffs the actual current team and grants Vigilance. Loan Shark observes real own spell history with an intervening-if guard. Buzzard's morbid counter is an entry replacement; Rictus's morbid token is a guarded entry trigger. Rise counts actual creature cards, excluding tokens and noncreature cards, and uses its complete green2/1 Varmint recipe. The separate Zombie Rogue recipe is blue/black2/2.

Stingerback Terror's printed7/7 applies outside the battlefield. Its battlefield-only -1/-1 per card in its controller's Hand uses the shared concrete/virtual stat evaluator. A confirmed preflight interaction requires prospective casting characteristics: a Hand spell leaves Hand before targets and total costs are chosen (CR601.2a/c/f). Borrowed target and cost-reduction queries omit only that exact proposed Hand object, without cloning Game or its arena. Actual announcements already have the source on Stack. Plot's payment and discard-source activation queries keep their actual Hand objects until payment. This correction covers recursive target predicates, direct/staged/multi-target proposals and target-dependent cost completion, alongside fixed and scalar live-power reductions. No mutable query context or extra persistent field is used.
