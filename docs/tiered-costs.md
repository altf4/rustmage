Fire Magic, Ice Magic, Restoration Magic, Thunder Magic, Tifa's Limit Break and
Vincent's Limit Break. This initial audit admits no body. The full Standard goal
remains unfinished. Foundation17d100b supplies reviewed timestamped base stats and

addition to the casting cost. Declaration precedes targets, mana abilities and
