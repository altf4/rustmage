# Adventure spell faces (issue254, foundation checkpoint)

This isolated author branch starts at the all-three-feature integration `7379c8558b0adbb95e019fac697a6c290d9e85ee` (1,760 Standard bodies/schema76). The first compiling foundation adds nineteen complete Adventure pairs to the compiler, for provisional **1,779 Standard identities, 84 token recipes and schema77**. Full paid public/Standard acceptance, recursive pinned-source manifest, canonical comparison, broad regressions and two independent nonauthor reviews remain required. This is a draft implementation, not a merge/readiness claim.

One physical parent Card/ObjectRef remains in objects and zones. Immutable internal inset definitions provide the selected spell's name, printed cost/MV, colors, type/subtypes, targets and program. They cannot be submitted as separate cards even in Freeform. Existing physical ordinary IDs stay in place; internal definition and token recipe IDs are catalogue-fingerprint scoped. Normal face aliases and original whole-card names both identify the physical parent; inset aliases identify non-deck helper definitions.

`Action::AnnounceAdventure { card }` chooses inset characteristics before timing, targets and funding. `CastInfo.face` (`Normal`/`Adventure`) is independent of `CastInfo.method` (`Normal`/`Flashback`/`Plot`/`Warp`). Inset casts retain their canonical parent in `CastInfo.physical_card`; `SpellView.source_card` is the chosen immutable definition. Ordinary cast metadata uses physical_card=None/face=Normal. This foundation permits ordinary paid inset casting from Hand and independently granted generic exile permissions; it does not reinterpret Adventure's own normal-only permission as another inset grant. Unimplemented intrinsic cost-selector/inset combinations remain closed in the whole-pair compiler.

Stack kind/color/MV predicates and cast observers use selected definitions. Live damage facts, source keywords and captured LKI use the selected inset rather than a main-face creature's properties. Generic reductions and cost locking share the chosen definition while retaining exact physical source exclusion from prospective Hand queries. The complete normal permanent/land programs remain on the physical parent for later entry, activation and combat.

Successful inset resolution moves the same exact object to face-up Exile, then creates an indefinite snapshot-owned `AdventurePermission { object, card, player }`. Countering or all-target-illegal fizzle creates none. Its entitled player pays normal costs under normal timing to play the main face, including real land-play limits/tapped entry for Midgar. Leaving that exact exile incarnation removes the permission; clone/restore/parent closure preserve it independently. No next-turn delay, forced-any-departure exile, or free-cost permission is inherited from Warp/flashback/Plot. Empty games have only an optional pointer; live permission vectors and nested state are counted as owned memory.

The nineteen full pairs are Bilbo Baggins, Burglar; Frolicking Familiar; Galvanic Giant; Gingerbread Hunter; Glóin the Mighty; Gollum, Silent Slinker; Grabby Giant; Hollow Scavenger; Imodane's Recruiter; Midgar, City of Mako; Minecart Daredevil; Obyra's Attendants; Ratcatcher Trainee; Shrouded Shepherd; Smaug, the Great Calamity; Stormkeld Vanguard; Tempest Hart; Threadbind Clique; Two-Headed Hunter. Complete original face texts are retained in the pinned snapshot. Whole-pair admission requires both genuine face bodies to compile, not a name whitelist or a partially executed parent. The other56 Adventure parents and all13 Omen parents remain gated. Omen's separate shuffle-on-resolution behavior belongs to issue255.
