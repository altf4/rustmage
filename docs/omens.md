# Omen spell faces

All thirteen pinned Standard Omen identities now compile as complete physical pairs, including both normal and inset bodies. The [full-face manifest](omen-admissions.json) tracks their source provenance. The combined candidate supports 1,817/5,164 Standard identities, with 3,347 still gated, 87 token recipes, one emblem, nineteen Adventure helpers and thirteen Omen helpers (5,303 definitions), schema80. This candidate is under validation and independent review; it does not complete Standard support.

[Chosen-spell timing](cast-timing.md), [selected-face X](selected-face-x.md), [same-graveyard target groups](single-graveyard.md) and [split search destinations](split-library-search.md) record the four general kernels. The latter two also admit Arashin Sunshield, Griffnaut Tracker and Troop of Ponies. Complete Omen pairs: Bloomvine Regent, Dirgur Island Dragon, Disruptive Stormbrood, Feral Deathgorger, Marang River Regent, Purging Stormbrood, Riling Dawnbreaker, Runescale Stormbrood, Sagu Wildling, Scavenger Regent, Stormshriek Feral, Twinmaw Stormbrood and Whirlwing Stormbrood.

One physical card selects either its normal definition or its internal Omen spell definition. Chosen mana cost, colors, types, mana value, targets, source predicates and stack facts follow that face. Internal helpers are rejected as deck cards. The normal face's intrinsic abilities do not leak onto the inset.

Successful Omen resolution shuffles the actual card into its owner's library, using the owned game RNG and ordinary zone-change bookkeeping. Countering the spell or making all its targets illegal does not shuffle it or grant an Adventure permission. Sagu Wildling's search instruction shuffles first; its subsequent Omen completion performs the separately instructed second shuffle. Clones and snapshots own private continuations and preserve both RNG events independently.

Complete normal bodies include Ward and triggered/activated abilities: Marang returns two other nonland permanents; Purging removes all counters from its controlled target; Runescale observes noncreature or Dragon casts; Stormshriek performs mandatory discard followed by draw only if a card was discarded. [Whirlwing's timing record](cast-timing.md) explains its general Sorcery/Dragon casting permission, chosen-face behavior and preserved special-action restrictions.

The compiler authenticates the exact two Omen completion reminders and supported intrinsic reminders before parsing each complete face. Unknown parentheticals, residual instructions and incomplete faces keep the entire physical parent gated. The [nine-pair review record](review-pr-263.md) retains historical baseline validation and the composed-main evidence. Whirlwing's merged component evidence is recorded in [cast-timing.md](cast-timing.md); the authoritative combined source and gate status are in [review-pr-275.md](review-pr-275.md). These intermediate batches do not complete the full Standard goal.
