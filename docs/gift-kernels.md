The engine will declare the optional non-targeted opponent beneficiary before
choosing targets. Promised and declined declarations select distinct complete
target plans, including required gift-only targets and changed cardinalities.
Funding must consider either valid declaration before casting, then only the
chosen declaration while selecting targets and paying costs. Additional discard
costs remain casting costs, independent of later Gift creation.

For instants and sorceries, gift delivery runs before other spell effects only
when the real spell resolves. Countering the spell or making all its targets
illegal prevents the gift. Token replacements resume exactly once and belong
to the selected beneficiary. Four typed gifts are in scope: a card, Food,
Treasure, and a tapped1/1blue Fish. For permanents, the inherent Gift entry trigger
is separate from printed entry triggers. Both retain the paid cast's exact public
entry incarnation, beneficiary and controller; blinking or uncast entry cannot
inherit a promise, and source departure cannot rebind a queued trigger.
