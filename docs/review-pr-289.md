## Rules and complete bodies

The twelve consumers are Besotted Knight, Conceited Witch, Ferocious Werefox,
Vantress Transmuter, Charmed Clothier, Charming Scoundrel, Embereth Veteran,
Living Lectern, Redtooth Genealogist, Royal Treatment, Spiteful Hexmage and
Splashy Spellcaster. Every Adventure includes its complete normal body and exile
permission. Ordinary modal branches, activation payments/sacrifices, printed draw,
hexproof and trigger clauses are retained.

Token replacement completes before attachment. Replacement-added non-Role tokens
remain unattached. Illegal exact recipients produce no Role. Cursed sets base1/1
after characteristic-defining values and before additions/counters. Young Hero
has no flat bonus and rechecks its toughness condition on resolution. Royal ward,
Sorcerer scry and Young Hero triggers belong to the enchanted creature's
controller; Wicked's departure trigger belongs to the Aura's controller.

Each controller keeps its latest Role on an exact recipient; the other player's
Roles coexist. Simultaneous newest timestamps require a public exact-reference
choice. Role/legend choices, lethal deaths and counter cancellation share the
frozen APNAP state-based event. Registry entries, timestamps, replacement work,
private choices and captured granted abilities are owned by snapshots/clones.
