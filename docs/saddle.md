## Rules and API

[CR 702.171](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.pdf) defines a sorcery-speed activated ability whose cost taps any number of **other**, untapped creatures controlled by the player with combined power at least the printed threshold. Summoning sickness and the contributor's creature type do not restrict this non-targeting cost. The Mount itself may already be tapped or summoning sick.

The actor announces the ordinary activation, then receives `saddle_choice`. `ChooseSaddler { object }` records a current incarnation without tapping anything. `FinishSaddle` explicitly ends the contribution set after its signed total meets the threshold. Reaching the minimum does not force completion: zero, negative and excess contributions remain selectable whenever some completion remains legal. A choice is pruned only when the exact maximum attainable final total cannot reach the threshold. This needs linear scans and signed sums, with no subset enumeration.

Selected contributions remain private to the actor. Opponent observations expose neither the private set nor its legal continuations. Views report the threshold, selected exact references, `selected_power`, and exact `can_finish`. Each individual creature's supported power is `i128`. Aggregation uses signed magnitude with a widened carry, so multiple extreme values can cancel correctly. `selected_power: null` means only that the aggregate cannot fit `i128`; it never implies an error or invalid cost. `can_finish` and action legality remain exact. Printed Saddle thresholds in this admission grammar are fixed integers 1 through 65,535; variable, zero and composite Saddle declarations stay gated.

Finishing commits all taps atomically and stacks the ordinary activation. Genuine untapped-to-tapped trigger packets are queued above it. There is no invented zero-mana payment window. Costs remain paid if the Saddle ability is countered. Only resolution marks the exact surviving source incarnation saddled. Departure/reentry cannot mark the replacement object. Sparse owned references carry the designation; cleanup and battlefield departure clear it. There is no new per-permanent field in stored objects.

Attack-while-saddled conditions are captured when attackers are committed. They are not resolution-time intervening conditions. Immutable queued programs survive source departure, while a source-directed effect still requires its original incarnation. Burrowfiend triggers on the first absent-to-marked resolution for that object in the turn; additional Saddle activations remain legal but do not retrigger it.

The ordinary public action transaction protects private selections, all cost taps, RNG, trigger identity allocation, mana, markers and observations against late capacity failures. Native coverage includes failure while allocating a tap-trigger packet after the selected costs begin committing.
