# Complete own-graveyard recovery grammar

Issue #285 adds eighteen complete Standard card bodies using the existing typed engine. The compiler recognizes exact own-graveyard moves to hand, library top or library bottom, with explicit cardinality, positive card kinds, permanent/legendary/subtype predicates and bounded mana value. Target keys are deterministic hashes of typed semantics; no card name authorizes admission. Existing exact productions take precedence to preserve their generated programs.

The admitted cards are Archenemy's Charm, Auroral Procession, Barkform Harvester, Coati Scavenger, Dundoolin Weaver, Elvish Regrower, Fateshaper Aspirant, Fight On!, Hazel's Nocturne, Macabre Waltz, Oltec Archaeologists, Pull from the Grave, Restore with Empathy, Rydia's Return, Tomb Trawler, Tombstone, Career Criminal, True Ancestry and White Widow, Free Agent. Their complete modes, triggers, activated abilities, keywords, static effects and follow-up instructions compile together. The mandatory recovery-then-discard production lowers to recovery followed by discard, including zero targets and the ordinary all-targets-illegal fizzle rule.

Raw parentheticals in this semantic family must match their actual production and position before any historical text extractor can hide them. Intrinsic keywords, prowess, fixed kicker/bargain, the implemented forage reflexive production, target-granted indestructible, parameter-matched scry and the actual Clue recipe each authenticate their own slot. Unknown, nested, detached, infix, duplicated, stolen or mismatched reminders gate the whole body. Iterative erasure is used only to detect an attempted route, never to authenticate instructions. This closes recovery-family reminders; it does not claim the separate global reminder audit is complete.

The corpus inventory examined all 3,339 gated identities at main `0977111f57734f6e4e22bf6c3c68a88358d29da9`: 136 bodies mention own-graveyard recovery and 92 are shorter than 250 characters. An in-memory prototype suggested nineteen whole-body admissions. Paid native probes found that Morcant's Loyalist needs exact post-death source binding for its `another target Elf` exclusion. It remains gated, and issue #287 tracks that runtime dependency. The new grammar consequently declines `another target` recovery. Existing Bonebind Orator and Theoretical Necromancer graveyard activations remain byte-identical; their sources exile as a cost rather than creating this death-trigger case.

Other excluded obligations include private mill-then-select recovery, negative card kinds, shared-subtype target groups, exile targets and unrecorded event/threshold histories. These are runtime dependencies, not clauses to erase. No new engine semantics or ABI fields are introduced.
